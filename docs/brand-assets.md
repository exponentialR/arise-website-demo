# Brand assets and approval status

The demonstrator uses locally stored copies of identity marks obtained from official university or funder websites. They are shown as separate attributed identities, not combined into a new ARISE logo. Inclusion in this prototype does not imply endorsement or settle the approval, ordering or clear-space rules for an official consortium site.

Before launch, the consortium should ask each brand owner to confirm the asset variant and intended use. Do not recolour, redraw, distort or combine these marks.
