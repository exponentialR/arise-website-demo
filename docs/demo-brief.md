# Demo meeting story

1. Show the live homepage.
2. Open Research and explain the connected areas of enquiry.
3. Open ConPose and show its authors, funding evidence and related profiles.
4. Show one content record to demonstrate maintenance.
5. Show the GitHub repository and automated deployment workflow.
6. Explain the three URL/cost routes.
7. Ask for decisions on branding, ownership, editorial responsibility and domain route.

The working walkthrough is documented in [`demo-script.md`](demo-script.md).
