# Funding and publication evidence audit

Last checked: 2 September 2026.

## Purpose and scope

This demonstrator catalogues outputs that could be connected to an ARISE award through a public primary or publisher source. The audit searched the exact identifiers `2222815`, `21/US/3797` and `USI-218`, checked the official NSF award product list, and followed DOI, publisher, proceedings and public-access records.

The result is a dated public-source audit—not a guarantee of completeness. Consortium investigators and funders should review the catalogue before it is described as the programme's definitive publication list.

## Evidence categories

Each funding relationship carries one of three labels:

1. **Acknowledged by publication** — the paper or author manuscript names the award.
2. **Publisher funding metadata** — the publisher-deposited metadata names the award.
3. **Reported by funder** — the official funder record lists the work as an award output.

The site exposes these distinctions rather than silently treating them as equivalent.

- DOI and publisher records linked from each publication page
- NSF Public Access Repository copies where used as paper-level evidence
- Official ISEC and ISARC proceedings records for the relevant conference papers

## Deliberate limits

- An output is linked to `isc-xr` only when that narrower relationship is supported; wider award outputs are not forced onto the project page.
- The catalogue does not imply that the ARISE award was an output's only support.
- Exact author names are retained even when the demonstrator has no verified person profile for that author.
- The engagement record omits presenter and exact session details where the official source does not provide them.
- Official-source institution and funder marks are included provisionally for the demonstrator. Their sources and approval caveats are recorded in `docs/brand-assets.md`; final programme use remains subject to the relevant brand owners and consortium review.
